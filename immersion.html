Technical Experience Overview

Software and Peripheral Support

  • Repaired and charged handheld meter reading devices used for field readings across various service areas
  • Shadowed experienced programmers during critical system updates in sub-offices (e.g., Gonzaga branch)
  • Digitally archived important company files and ensured proper labeling of physical documents for easy retrieval
  • Performed software diagnostics and troubleshooting on handheld devices to ensure accurate meter readings

Hardware Maintenance

  • Used compressed air (blower) to remove dust from computer components and printers to prevent overheating and extend equipment lifespan
  • Cleaned system unit interiors including CPU fans, power supplies, and motherboard components
  • Maintained printer mechanisms by removing paper dust and debris from internal components
  • Applied proper cleaning techniques to prevent static damage to sensitive electronic components

Workstation Installation & Setup

  • Helped set up workstations by installing system units, monitors, keyboards, and peripherals while ensuring proper cable management
  • Configured BIOS settings and verified hardware compatibility before OS installation
  • Implemented cable management best practices to ensure clean, organized, and safe workspaces
  • Tested all peripherals and connections to ensure full functionality before deployment

Printer Maintenance & Troubleshooting

  • Assisted in troubleshooting dot matrix printers, including cleaning print heads to ensure clear output
  • Replaced printer ribbons and aligned paper feeders for optimal print quality
  • Diagnosed and resolved common printer issues such as paper jams, misalignment, and connectivity problems
  • Performed regular maintenance checks to prevent future malfunctions

Network Installation & Configuration

  • Created straight-through and crossover cables by attaching RJ45 connectors to Cat5e cables following T568A and T568B standards
  • Tested cable connections using cable testers to ensure proper wiring and signal integrity
  • Learned proper crimping techniques to create reliable network connections
  • Assisted in organizing and labeling network cables for efficient cable management

Audio-Visual Technical Support

  • Operated sound systems independently during company events, ensuring clear audio quality
  • Set up microphones, speakers, and audio mixers for corporate meetings and presentations
  • Troubleshot audio feedback and connectivity issues during live events
  • Managed audio equipment inventory and ensured proper storage after events

Reflection as a CSS Student

This immersion reinforced my technical skills and emphasized the importance of:

  • Attention to Detail: Proper cable management and device handling prevent system failures and ensure long-term reliability
  • Problem-Solving: Diagnosing printer issues and network problems improved my analytical skills and troubleshooting methodologies
  • Adaptability: Learning to configure unfamiliar equipment (e.g., sound systems, meter reading devices) expanded my versatility as an IT professional
  • Teamwork: Collaborating with senior technicians enhanced my communication skills and taught me the value of knowledge sharing
  • Professional Ethics: Understanding the importance of data security and proper handling of company equipment and information
